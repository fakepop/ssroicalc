What is fixed and what can be changed. IR fees run at list, and the plan discount and included hours hold whatever the incident, so the IR fee saving is contractual. Business loss, the response-speed reduction, and the hours saved by knowing the environment are estimates that will vary from one organisation to another. Most serious incidents begin outside business hours, which is why the default is the after hours rate. This said, victims we support should expect a blended rate. The risk-adjusted view weights a single year by the annual chance of an incident. The no-incident credit is value toward other StreamScan services, not cash back.
